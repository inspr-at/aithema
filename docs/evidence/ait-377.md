## Provider-owned integration gaps

1. **S1 and grant-relative deadlines are not wired in the current clients.** Neither client exposes microphone acquisition/grant phases. The rail accepts `options.onMicrophoneState('requesting' | 'granted')`, and deferred-port tests prove the UI timer behavior, including a 60 s prompt and a grant at 20 s failing at 50 s. Current clients do not call it, and their internal `voiceOperation`/credential scopes retain their own deadlines. Production S1 and the requested S1 → cleanup/cooldown → connect ordering therefore remain blocked on provider-owned work. Do not interpret the synthetic S1 screenshot as proof of that integration. The implementation does not pre-acquire a second microphone or wrap global media APIs.
2. **Audio-only replies and filler need a qualified reply event.** ElevenLabs `onModeChange` emits `speaking` without proving audio; assistant `final` events do carry text. The rail's 15 s watchdog uses non-filler assistant transcripts, cancels while a visitor partial is in progress, rearms on a user final or typed submission, and never rearms from Listening/visitor silence. Speaking mode alone does not clear it. It ignores a supplied `filler` flag and the specified “Einen Moment, bitte.” phrase, but current clients do not provide general filler provenance. An audio-only late answer cannot safely clear it until the client supplies that signal. No provider files were edited.
3. **Agent-end charge carry-in is handled conservatively.** The native ElevenLabs `ended` event has no usage. The rail now retains the ended call identity and shows pending charge copy until matching server settlement supplies confirmed usage. If a future event carries a confirmed terminal receipt it can settle immediately. No new client usage event is required to keep today's copy truthful.
